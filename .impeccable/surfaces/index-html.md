# Surface: GrowLine farmer registration (index.html)

Mode: Persuade, then Operate (the registration form). Audience: Ogun State tomato farmers on low-end Android phones, outdoors in sun. Action: register a farm in about 3 minutes. Proof: the example SMS itself; no stats or testimonials exist. Constraints: copy verbatim from the team doc; light on 3G; nothing essential depends on images, fonts or the map.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the SMS arriving in the field: a real farmer in a real crop with GrowLine's message emerging from the bottom edge of the frame. It refuses the stock agritech hero with an icon-card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Inset photo frames (28px radius) floating on warm paper #F6F2E9. Deep leaf #1B5E34 and night #0E1711 panels, a tomato-red #C2311B action colour and a full red band, lime #D3EC6B highlights with dark text, and pill CTAs with a lime arrow disc. Bricolage Grotesque for display, system sans for body.
STORY: The farmer sees advice that saves money. They learn what GrowLine is and why it asks, see 3 steps on a red band, register on a dark panel with a white form, and get answers in the FAQ. The page ends with a giant GrowLine wordmark.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-height inset photo of a farmer tending crops. A white logo and a "Register my farm" link sit at the top, the white headline at clamp(40px, 11vw, 92px) is bottom-left over a dark gradient, then the subhead and a red pill CTA. A phone with the SMS rises out of the frame's bottom edge (bottom-right on desktop, centre-bottom on mobile).
FORM: The user-pinned blend of the AgriSync layout and the Tomato site's red bands. The direction was pinned by the brief, so there was no roll and no seed key (seed: user-pinned).
SIGNATURE: Once, on load, the phone rises 24px and fades in, typing dots show for 900ms, then the message bubble appears. Reduced motion shows the final state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
